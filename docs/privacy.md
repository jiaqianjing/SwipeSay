# SwipeSay 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026-11-19

## 中文

SwipeSay（包括 macOS、iPhone 和 iPad 版本）是一个由个人开发者维护的翻译与学习工具。**SwipeSay 没有自己的服务器，开发者不收集、不接收、也不保存你的任何内容或个人信息。**

### 你的文字会发送到哪里

翻译、单词卡、重点表达、句子解析和追问，都由**你自己选择并配置**的模型服务生成，例如 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini、DeepSeek，或你填写的任意 OpenAI 兼容服务。使用这些功能时，以下内容会从你的设备**直接**发送到该服务：

- 你选中或输入的文字；
- 在启用「结合上下文理解」时，所选文字所在的段落（Mac 上通过辅助功能读取；iPhone / iPad 上仅在 Safari 中通过分享菜单读取）；
- 你提出的追问，以及同一次对话中之前的问答；
- 你的语言和学习水平设置（用于生成合适的解释）。

在 iPhone 和 iPad 上，首次使用某个模型服务前，SwipeSay 会明确告诉你内容将发送给哪个服务、哪个地址，并征得你的同意。你可以随时在 设置 › 隐私 中撤回。

这些服务如何处理你的数据，以它们各自的隐私政策和服务条款为准。

### 保存在你设备上的数据

- **API Key**：保存在系统钥匙串中，只在请求对应服务时使用。
- **生词本、翻译缓存、用量统计**：只保存在你的设备上（iPhone / iPad 上保存在 App 与其分享扩展共用的 App Group 容器中）。不会上传到开发者，也不会被同步到其他地方。删除 App 即会删除这些数据。

### 不做的事

- 不使用任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK；
- 不追踪你在其他 App 或网站中的行为；
- 不出售或共享你的数据。

### 儿童

SwipeSay 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 联系

如有问题，请在 GitHub 提交 issue：<https://github.com/jiaqianjing/SwipeSay/issues>

## English

SwipeSay (macOS, iPhone and iPad) is a translation and language-learning tool maintained by an independent developer. **SwipeSay has no servers of its own. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your content or personal information.**

### Where your text goes

Translations, word cards, key expressions, sentence analysis and follow-up answers are generated by the AI service **you choose and configure**, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek or any OpenAI-compatible server you enter. When you use these features, the following is sent **directly** from your device to that service:

- the text you selected or typed;
- with "use context" turned on, the paragraph around your selection (read through Accessibility on the Mac; on iPhone and iPad only in Safari, through the share sheet);
- your follow-up questions and the earlier questions and answers in the same conversation;
- your language and learner-level settings, so explanations fit you.

On iPhone and iPad, before a service is used for the first time, SwipeSay tells you which service and which host will receive your text and asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy.

How those services handle your data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

### Data kept on your device

- **API keys** are stored in the system keychain and only used for requests to the matching service.
- **Vocabulary, translation cache and usage statistics** stay on your device (on iPhone and iPad, in the App Group container shared by the app and its share extension). They are never uploaded to the developer. Deleting the app deletes them.

### What SwipeSay does not do

- No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.
- No selling or sharing of your data.

### Children

SwipeSay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
